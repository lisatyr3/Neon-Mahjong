How to Play

  • Match two free tiles with the same corner symbol and color.
  • A tile is free if nothing sits on top of it and at least one side (left or right) is open.
  • Chain matches within 4 seconds of each other to build Heat — each level adds bonus points and bonus time.
  • Run out of moves and the board auto-shuffles for a small time penalty.
  • Clear the board before time runs out for a big speed bonus!
